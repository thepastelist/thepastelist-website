Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you customise hampers?

Yes. Every gift can be customised around the occasion, the person you’re gifting, what they love and your budget.

What is the starting price?

Hampers start from ₹499. The final price depends on the items you choose and the personalisation.

Can I choose my budget?

Yes. Tell us your preferred budget and we’ll curate a gift around it. If a quote feels high, we’ll happily rework the contents rather than compromise on quality.

Do you deliver in Kochi?

Yes. Surprise doorstep delivery is available in Kochi and nearby areas, subject to serviceability. Pickup can also be arranged.

Do you ship across India?

Yes, courier shipping is available across India.

Do you ship internationally?

International shipping is available where supported. Delivery time and charges depend on the destination.

Can I add a handwritten letter?

Yes. Handwritten letters can be added to any gift, or ordered on their own. Not sure what to write? Tell us the feeling and we’ll help you put it into words.

Can I see the hamper before delivery?

Where applicable, you can preview and approve the final arrangement before dispatch. We also confirm names, spellings, dates and messages with you before we make anything personalised.

How early should I place my order?

Lead time depends on the type of gift, personalisation and delivery location. Contact us for availability. For last-minute gifts, message us anyway and we’ll check what’s possible.

How can I place an order?

Message us on WhatsApp, or use the form above, with your occasion, budget and requirements. We’ll suggest options and share a quote, and your order is confirmed once payment is received.

Can I order just a card, frame or album?

Yes. Handwritten letters, wish cards, frames, albums and polaroids can all be ordered on their own.

Do you take bulk and corporate orders?

We do: return gifts, farewell and teacher gifts, and gifts for teams and clients. Tell us the quantity and date and we’ll put together options.
