Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does a hamper cost?

Our hampers start from around ₹499. The final price depends on what goes inside, the size and the personalisation. Share your budget and we’ll curate within it. If a quote feels high, we’ll happily rework the contents rather than compromise on quality.

Can I choose what goes inside?

Absolutely. Pick products you like, tell us your colours or theme, or simply tell us about the person and let us curate the rest. Every hamper is customised.

Can I order just a letter, card or frame?

Yes. Handwritten letters, wish cards, frames, albums and polaroids can all be ordered on their own. You don’t need to buy a hamper.

Do you deliver outside Kochi?

Yes! We offer direct surprise delivery in and around Kochi, and courier shipping across India and worldwide. Pickup can also be arranged.

How early should I order?

As early as you can, since personalised pieces take time to make well. For last-minute orders, message us anyway: the choices may be fewer, but we’ll check what’s possible and do our best.

Will I see my gift before it’s sent?

Yes. We share names, spellings, dates and messages for your approval before we make anything, and send you a little preview before dispatch.

How do I pay?

Once you’re happy with the combination and the quote, we share payment details on WhatsApp. Your order is confirmed as soon as payment is received.

Do you take bulk and corporate orders?

We do: return gifts, farewell and teacher gifts, employee and client gifting, and wedding favours. Tell us the quantity and date and we’ll put together options.
